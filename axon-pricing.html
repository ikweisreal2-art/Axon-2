Common Questions
How quickly can I get an agent live? +
Most clients are live within 48 hours of completing the onboarding form. Complex enterprise setups may take up to 5 business days.
Can I switch plans later? +
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply at the next billing cycle.
What happens when I hit my conversation limit? +
Your agent stays live but you'll receive an alert. You can upgrade your plan or purchase additional conversation credits. We never cut off your agent without warning.
Do I need to install anything? +
No. AXON agents deploy via a single line of JavaScript you paste into your website. No plugins, no complex setup.
Is there a contract or minimum commitment? +
No contracts. All plans are month-to-month. Annual plans are paid upfront for a 20% discount but can be cancelled with a prorated refund.
